**Short name:** Robots Among Us

**Workshop:** January 5, 2027, Hilton Waikoloa Village, Big Island, Hawaiʻi. Assigned time and room remain pending. HICSS lists morning and afternoon workshop blocks; neither is an assignment to this workshop.

## Start here

1. Connor fills the initial prospect list with fewer than 12 people and assigns one sender to each. Verify one research connection for every personal invitation.

## The weekly hour

Spend 25 minutes on invitations, 20 minutes on follow-ups, and 15 minutes on submissions and tracking. Acknowledge every submission within one week. Send only one follow-up to unanswered initial invitations, after seven to ten days; subsequent reminders require expressed interest or an agreed writing date.

## When recruitment stalls

If replies are scarce, seek introductions and make the research connection more specific. If people express interest but do not submit, ask what is blocking completion and agree on a writing date. If six suitable contributors are unavailable, ask a willing, appropriate contributor about a second panel. If three substantive debates still cannot be formed, run two stronger panels and extend audience discussion.

# Select and confirm the panels

After November 15, compare the completed statements and their authors' stated positions. Look for a meaningful disagreement about the same question, clear reasoning, and confirmed availability. Do not require simple “for” and “against” labels when the real contrast is conditional. Ask the authors to clarify ambiguous positions before pairing them.

Use the worksheet on page 4. Have the organizers review proposed pairings and assign a moderator. A submitted statement, an expression of interest, and a confirmed panel place are different statuses. Only confirmed contributors should be named publicly.
